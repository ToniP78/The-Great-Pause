Why The Great Pause

Hormonal transitions are a natural part of life — yet too many people move through them without enough information, understanding or support.

Too often, the conversation happens quietly — if it happens at all.

The Great Pause exists to change that.

What we're building

A global movement, built on four commitments.

Education

Making credible, evidence-informed information easier to understand and access.

Conversation

Creating spaces where hormonal health can be discussed openly and without shame.

Connection

Helping people find appropriate resources, professionals and support.

Advocacy

Encouraging better awareness, healthcare access, workplace understanding and public dialogue.

Who it's for

This conversation is for everyone.
